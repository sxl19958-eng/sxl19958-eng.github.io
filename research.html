RESEARCH

Research

My research develops analytical and data-driven methods to address complex decisions under uncertain demand, limited information, and evolving operational conditions.

01

Data-Driven & Distributionally Robust Optimization

Developing decision frameworks that integrate observed demand information, data correction, and distributionally robust optimization. Applications include censored-demand newsvendor problems, inventory decisions, production planning, and reverse logistics.

Distributionally robust optimizationDemand censoringInventory controlNewsvendor models

Related work: IJPE (2025) · EJOR (2024)

02

Supply Chain Efficiency & Resilience

Studying how organizational capabilities, inter-firm networks, and information structures shape supply chain performance. Research approaches span empirical analysis, network-based modeling, and configuration methods.

Supply chain resilienceGovernance networksOperational efficiencyInformation sharing

Related work: IJLRA (2026) · IJLRA (2026)

03

Demand Forecasting, AI & Information Fusion

Integrating predictive methods with operational decision-making, including multidimensional spatiotemporal networks for bike-sharing demand and dynamic fusion for new-product life-cycle prediction.

Spatiotemporal networksProduct life cyclesMachine learningDynamic forecasting
