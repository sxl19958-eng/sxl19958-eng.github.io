About Me

I am Xiaoli Su (苏晓丽), a researcher affiliated with the School of Economics and Management at Fuzhou University. My research explores how data, analytics, and optimization can improve operational decisions under uncertainty.

My work focuses on data-driven and distributionally robust optimization, demand forecasting, inventory control, and supply chain analytics. I am especially interested in combining machine learning, information fusion, and mathematical modeling to support resilient and responsive operations.

I received my Ph.D. in Management, specializing in Management Systems Engineering, from Fuzhou University in 2025. My international research training included emlyon business school in Lyon and De Vinci Higher Education in Paris.
